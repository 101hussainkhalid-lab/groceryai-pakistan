Meal Planner Grocery Guide

Pakistan pantry essentials

Make your weekly list feel local, useful, and realistic.

From Lahore’s bustling markets to Karachi supermarkets, the best grocery list is one that matches the way families actually cook at home.

Staples

Atta, rice, daal, onions, potatoes, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, green chilies.

Protein

Chicken, beef mince, eggs, yogurt, paneer, and lentils for fast everyday meals.

Fresh extras

Lemon, coriander, mint, cucumbers, carrots, and seasonal greens for quick sides.

Sample family list

  • 2 kg basmati rice
  • 5 kg atta
  • 3 varieties of daal
  • Chicken thighs
  • Beef mince
  • Yogurt and eggs
  • Onions, potatoes, tomatoes, lemons
  • Spices: turmeric, cumin, chili, coriander
